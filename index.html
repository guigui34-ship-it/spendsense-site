How it works

  1. 1

    Log a purchase

    Add it in two seconds, or let SpendSense spot your order on supported stores.

  2. 2

    Use it for a while

    A reminder arrives 7, 30 and 90 days later, once you actually know.

  3. 3

    Rate it honestly

    One to five stars, plus a note on what made it worth it — or not.

  4. 4

    Learn your patterns

    See which kinds of purchases make you happy and which you regret.

Rate later

Rate it once you've actually used it

The excitement of buying fades. SpendSense asks again after a week, a month and three months, when your opinion is honest.

  • One-tap star ratings with undo
  • Short notes on what made it worth it
  • Reminders you can snooze or turn off
Automatic detection

Spots your orders for you

When you finish an order on a supported store, a small card offers to log it. Check the name and total, then track it in one click.

  • Amazon, Fnac, Decathlon, Zalando, Etsy, AliExpress, Cdiscount, Apple, Booking.com
  • Finds the order total, not the subtotal
  • Never appears on product or cart pages
