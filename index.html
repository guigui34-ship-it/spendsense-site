Find out which purchases were worth it

SpendSense is a free Chrome extension that tracks what you buy and how you feel about it weeks later — so you buy less and enjoy more.

Add to Chrome — it's free No account. No servers. Your data stays in your browser.
🛒

Log in seconds

Add a purchase by hand, or let SpendSense spot your order on Amazon, Fnac, Decathlon, Zalando and other supported stores.

⭐

Rate it later

Reminders 7, 30 and 90 days after each purchase ask one question: was it worth the money?

📊

Learn your patterns

See your best buys, your regrets, how much went to purchases you regret, and where your money goes.

🔒

Private by design

Everything is stored locally in your browser. Back up, export to CSV or delete your data anytime.
